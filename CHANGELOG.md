## 1.0.0
---
- First release for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge), ported from the NeoForge 1.21.1 version with full feature parity
- Gate spell casting and inscription behind Pufferfish's Skills skills, configured by datapack (`spell_skill_requirements/`)
- Grant innate spells from skills (`innate_spells/`): cast without a spellbook via keybind, with a spell wheel and a selected-spell badge left of the offhand slot
- Upgrade innate spells by chaining skill nodes at higher levels; only the highest level shows in the wheel
- Innate spells now also resync after respawning, so Pufferfish's erase-on-death and data imports are reflected immediately
- Requires Forge 47.4+, Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 1.20.1-3.1.4+, and Pufferfish's Skills 0.18+
